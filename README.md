# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Business Problem
A company is experiencing employee turnover. This project identifies the patterns
associated with employee attrition and recommends retention strategies.

## Key Findings
- **Overall attrition rate:** 16.10% (161 of 1,000 employees)
- **First-year risk:** 58.39% of leavers exited at 0 years of service
- **Overtime impact:** 23.79% of overtime employees left vs. 13.27% without overtime
- **No gender gap:** Attrition is evenly split between male and female employees
